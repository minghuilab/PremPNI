## prempni_dna_example_output.tsv and prempni_rna_example_output.tsv

- Type: The interaction type (`Protein-DNA` or `Protein-RNA`).

- Wild-type protein sequence: The wild-type protein sequence in one-letter amino acid codes.

- Nucleic acid sequence: The DNA or RNA sequence; multiple chains are separated by `&`.

- Mutation: The amino acid substitution.

- Predicted ΔΔG (kcal/mol): The predicted change in binding free energy upon mutation.

- Predicted effect: Stabilizing (ΔΔG < 0) or Destabilizing (ΔΔG ≥ 0).
